## 🚀 **HOW TO USE**

### **From Your App:**
1. Navigate to the **Locations/Places** tab
2. Choose your view mode (Grid/List/Map)
3. Search or filter locations
4. Tap any location for details
5. Use floating buttons for:
   - Tour Guides 👨‍🏫
   - Plan Trip 📅

### **To View a Location:**
1. Tap any location card
2. See full details, photos, and information
3. Tap "View in AR" for AR experience
4. Use "Get Directions" (ready for navigation)
5. Share with "Share" button

### **To Find a Tour Guide:**
1. Tap the "Tour Guides" floating button
2. Filter by specialty if needed
3. View guide profiles
4. Tap "Call" or "Email" to contact

### **To Plan a Trip:**
1. Tap the "Plan Trip" floating button
2. Choose "Tour Packages" tab for pre-made tours
3. OR choose "Custom Plan" to select locations
4. Select locations you want to visit
5. Tap "Generate" to create itinerary
