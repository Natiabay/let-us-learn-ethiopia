## 📊 **ETHIOPIAN LOCATIONS INCLUDED**

### **Historical Sites:**
1. **Rock-Hewn Churches of Lalibela**
   - 11 monolithic churches carved from rock
   - 12th century construction
   - UNESCO World Heritage Site
   - Spiritual center for Orthodox Christians

2. **Axum Obelisks & Ancient Kingdom**
   - Ancient Kingdom of Axum ruins
   - Home of Queen of Sheba legend
   - Towering obelisks
   - UNESCO World Heritage Site

3. **Gondar Castles (Fasil Ghebbi)**
   - 17th-century royal fortress
   - Medieval architecture
   - Multiple castles and palaces
   - UNESCO World Heritage Site

4. **Debre Damo Monastery**
   - 6th-century cliff-top monastery
   - Accessible only by rope
   - Ancient manuscripts and artifacts

### **Natural Sites:**
5. **Simien Mountains National Park**
   - Dramatic landscapes and peaks
   - Endemic wildlife (Gelada baboons, Ethiopian wolves)
   - UNESCO World Heritage Site
   - Excellent hiking and trekking

6. **Bale Mountains National Park**
   - High-altitude plateau
   - Ethiopian wolves and mountain nyala
   - 280+ bird species
   - Diverse ecosystems

7. **Danakil Depression**
   - One of hottest places on Earth
   - Active volcanoes
   - Colorful sulfur springs
   - Salt mining

8. **Lake Tana & Blue Nile Falls**
   - Ethiopia's largest lake
   - Source of the Blue Nile
   - Island monasteries
   - Boat tours

### **Religious & Cultural Sites:**
9. **Harar Jugol**
   - Walled city with 99 mosques
   - 102 shrines
   - Fourth holy city of Islam
   - UNESCO World Heritage Site
   - Famous hyena feeding

10. **Konso Cultural Landscape**
    - Terraced hillsides
    - Wooden grave markers
    - UNESCO World Heritage Site
    - Living cultural traditions

11. **National Museum of Ethiopia**
    - Home to "Lucy" fossil (3.2 million years old)
    - Ethiopian art and artifacts
    - Cultural heritage collections
    - Addis Ababa

12. **Merkato Market**
    - Africa's largest open-air market
    - Traditional crafts and spices
    - Cultural experience
